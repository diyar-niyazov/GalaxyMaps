The problem

Space distances are numbers nobody can feel.

  • "Polaris is 433 light-years away." What does that actually mean for a trip?
  • Planetarium apps show the sky, not how far or how long.
  • But everyone already knows one spatial interface: the map. Search, Directions, compare modes, read the ETA.

What if the universe had directions?

The product

A mapping app for the universe

  • One continuous map from the Earth–Moon system to nearby galaxies
  • Search 618 objects and 109,000 stars
  • Place cards with sourced facts and images
  • Directions, travel modes, multi-stop trips, playback
  • Realistic and Atlas layers
Directions, honestly

Earth → Polaris: 433 light-years

433 yearsat light speed
7.7 million yrat Voyager 1's real speed (JPL, heliocentric)
437 yr / 62 yrat 0.99 c: Earth time / onboard (relativity)
3.4 monthsUSS Enterprise, warp 9 (fiction)
  • True 3D distance between catalog positions, with its uncertainty (± 6.4 ly)
  • Constant-speed comparison: no acceleration, gravity or target motion, and the app says so
  • "On the way" is computed, never guessed: adding Vega costs +2.3%
Real data, visible provenance

Every number has a source

  • NASA/JPL Horizons, SIMBAD, HYG v4.4, NASA Exoplanet Archive, cited papers
  • Parallaxes inverted only when the error is ≤ 20%; otherwise the object is "route unavailable", with the reason
  • No fake routes to cosmological objects (3C 273, TON 618)
  • Imagery labeled: Observed image · Scientific illustration · AI reconstruction
Navigation is orbits

Why not fly straight to Mars?

  • Idealized Hohmann transfer: ≈ 259 days
  • Mars must be about 44° ahead at launch, so windows open roughly every 26 months
  • Circular, coplanar orbits; assumptions shown on screen
  • An educational model, not a launch-ready mission
Technical depth

One map, 17 orders of magnitude

  • Float64, camera-relative rendering (Three.js) from 6,000 km to 10²¹ km
  • van Wijk–Nuij smooth zoom flights; ecliptic → galactic plane blend
  • 109k stars in one batched draw, colored by temperature
  • Reproducible data pipeline → 4.5 MB offline bundle
  • 47 unit tests (physics, frames, dataset) + headless browser smoke test
